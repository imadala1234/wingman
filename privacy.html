WingMan privacy

WingMan is a personal flight tracker built for private use by its owner, Surendra Madala.

When you sign in with Google, WingMan asks only for access to files it creates in your own Google Drive (the "WingMan" spreadsheet) and your email address. Your flights, travellers and passport details are stored in that spreadsheet and on your device. They are not sent to any server run by WingMan and are not shared with anyone.

You can sign out in Settings at any time, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
